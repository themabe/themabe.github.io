About

A little about me

My path to tech wasn't traditional. In 2019, I started on the warehouse floor at Amazon. From there, I transitioned into data analytics and completed software engineering apprenticeships at both Amazon and Microsoft LEAP before converting into a full-time engineering role.

Today, I build and modernize experiences for Azure Container Registry, one of the services that powers containerized applications in the cloud.

My work spans customer-facing portal development, extending Azure CLI capabilities, and leading incident response and service reliability efforts for the service.

Because I have worked across different domains and industries, I naturally gravitate toward translating complex technical concepts into clear solutions.

When I'm AFK, you'll find me painting with acrylics, creating digital content, or working on projects that combine technology and creativity.
